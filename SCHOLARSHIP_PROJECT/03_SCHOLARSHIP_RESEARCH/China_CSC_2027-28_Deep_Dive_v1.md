- Each university admits you **and** decides its own CSC Type B nominations.

**Suggested approach:**

- Apply to **3 universities**: one top-tier (DUT or UESTC) and two potentially more accessible (NUIST, Hohai).
- Request pre-admission letters in **Nov–Dec 2026**.
- Then file the Type A application through the MoE when the Ethiopian call appears.

**Avoid:** universities whose master's is taught only in Chinese, and Zhejiang University (Type B deadline 31 Dec in the last cycle; very selective).

# Document checklist (common to 2027/28 notices; confirm each one)

1. CSC online application form (campuschina.org, which opens in your browser) with the correct **agency number**.
2. Passport copy (valid well beyond the start date).
3. Notarised degree certificate and transcript, plus English translations if needed (yours are in English).
4. **Study plan or research proposal**: usually at least 800 words for master's. Draft in `09_APPLICATION_DOCUMENTS/China_CSC/`.
5. **Two recommendation letters** from professors or associate professors (your DTU lecturers).
6. **Pre-admission letter** from at least one university.
7. Foreigner Physical Examination Form (hospital in Addis).
8. Police clearance certificate (non-criminal record).
9. English evidence: an IELTS result or a medium-of-instruction letter from DTU (each university states its own rule).
