**Evidence codes:**

- **E1:** read on the official site
- **E2:** official site found by search but not opened here (open it in your browser before acting)
- **E3:** secondary source only

**Labels:**

- **A:** strong and realistic
- **B:** possible, needs evidence
- **C:** low probability
- **D:** not eligible

None of the new items reaches A. The honest picture: most fully funded master's routes outside your current portfolio require a GPA of 3.0 or "upper second class", so the realistic new options are the ones that weigh **work experience, nomination or need** alongside grades.

**Funding words used exactly:**

- **Full:** tuition + living + travel + insurance
- **Tuition + stipend:** living costs may be partly covered
- **Partial:** tuition only, or a small stipend
