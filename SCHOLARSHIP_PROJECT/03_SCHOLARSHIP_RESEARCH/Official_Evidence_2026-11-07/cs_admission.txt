Admission

Welcome to the CYBERSURE admission process. We are looking for motivated students with a strong academic background and passion for cybersecurity who want to join our world-class programme.

CYBERSURE is a prestigious Erasmus Mundus Joint Master Degree programme offering full scholarships and partial scholarships to exceptional candidates from around the world.

Our admission process is designed to identify candidates who demonstrate academic excellence, technical aptitude, and commitment to advancing the field of cybersecurity.

Previous Higher Education Institution and Degree

Applicants should hold a Bachelor degree* awarded by a university or a higher education institution.

[]

The awarding institution has to be a recognized part of the official national education system of the country, i.e., to be listed as a degree-granting higher education institution by the country where it is located or by a relevant international organization such as UNESCO.

[]

The scope of a relevant degree awarded by a higher education institution must be equivalent to 180 ECTS credits or at least three years of full-time study. The degree must give eligibility for Master's degree studies in the awarding country.

English Language Proficiency

Our language of instruction is English, and applicants are expected to have an excellent command of it. The accepted language tests and their minimum score requirements are listed below.

                                                                                         Minimum Total Score   Minimum Writing Score   Minimum score for each section
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- --------------------- ----------------------- --------------------------------
  IELTS (Academic)                                                                       6.5                   6.0                     -
  TOEFL iBT (Internet-based Test) *(when the exam has been completed before 21.1.2026)   92                    22                      -
  TOEFL iBT (Internet based Test) *(when the exam was taken on or after 21.1.2026)       4.5                   4.5                     4.5
  C1 Advanced (former Cambridge Certificate of Advanced English, CAE)                    A,B, or C             -                       -
  C2 Proficiency (former Cambridge Certificate of Proficiency in English, CPE)           A,B, C or C1          -                       -

Important

-   •The minimum requirements are very strict. If your scores do not fulfill the minimum score requirements, your application will not be processed further.
-   •You cannot combine scores from different test dates (e.g., TOEFL MyBest scores are not accepted).
-   •IELTS and TOEFL results are valid for two years starting from the test date. Test results from the tests taken on or after 16. November 2024 is acceptable in the 2027 application round.
-   •IELTS Online (Academic) and TOEFL iBT Home Edition are accepted in the 2027 admission.

Only the language tests listed above are accepted. For further information on language test result deadline and submitting your test scores for verification, see the deadline overview.

All questions regarding English tests should be sent to [email protected].

Exemptions from the English Language Test Requirement

Exemption 1

Applicants whose higher education degree has been taken at an English medium programme requiring physical on-site presence at a university in Australia, Canada, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the United States, an EU/EEA country and Switzerland.

-   • If the degree is completed in an EU/EEA country (except Ireland), Switzerland or Canada, the language of the degree must be stated unambiguously in the degree certificate or its appendix, or in the transcript of records or other official document issued by the awarding higher education institution.
-   • A minimum of one half of the degree must be completed in a country and higher education institution that meets the requirements for exempting the applicant from taking an English test.

Exemption 2

Degree students form the CYBERSURE Consortium universities (NTNU, Aalto, DTU, UT, EURECOM, KTH, TU Graz).

-   • A minimum of one half of the degree must be completed at a Consortium university.

Exemption 3

Applicants who have attended CYBERSURE Consortium universities (NTNU, Aalto, DTU, UT, EURECOM, KTH, TU Graz) as exchange students.

All other applicants are expected to present a valid English language test result without exceptions.

Application Documents

CYBERSURE's application process is conducted fully online. All application documents must be uploaded to the online application portal before the application deadline. Incomplete applications, or applications with missing documents, will not be considered. The application documents should clearly indicate what they contain. Please read instructions carefully.

[]

Post-Admission Requirements

CYBERSURE’s application process is conducted fully online.

-   * The application documents must be uploaded to the online application portal before the application deadline. This includes country-specific documents

-   * After the admissions results are published, the selected CYBERSURE students must submit certified (attested) copies of the bachelor’s degree certificate and transcript of records, by the given deadline.

Admission is conditional until certified hard copies of the documents have been received. The conditionally granted study right will be cancelled if the certified documents are not received as requested

[]

The certified documents will be compared to the documents uploaded to the application portal. During the admission phase, CYBERSURE Admissions Office will perform spot checks on educational documents with the issuing universities.

CYBERSURE's admission process consists of the following 6 steps. Note that you can complete the first two steps before our application period begins. Make sure to read all the instructions carefully.

Application Period 2026/2027 Intake

Submit during the application window. Ensure all required materials are ready before the deadline.

Application Steps

Step 1

[]

Study the admission requirements

Can start early

Before you begin the application process, check your compliance with our admission requirements.

-   ●Review all admission requirements carefully
-   ●Check if you need to provide Country Specific documents and start collecting them early
-   ●Ensure you meet the educational background criteria
-   ●Check English language proficiency requirements
-   ●Verify your bachelor's degree is from a recognized institution

Step 2

[]

Prepare for the admission

Can start early

If an English language test is required from you and you have not taken the test yet, book a seat for it immediately.

Important note for self-funded students: If you apply as self-funded, you are required to upload documentation of financing according to the Norwegian Visa regulations.

-   ●Applications without a valid English test result are not eligible and will not be processed further
-   ●Start collecting your application documents
-   ●In case you apply as a self-funded student, you are required to upload documentation of financing
-   ●Prepare your motivation video
-   ●Contact potential recommenders early
-   ●Reflect over your second year specialization preferences
-   ●If you intend to apply for a Special Needs scholarship, prepare a short application (maximum 200 words) as a separate PDF.

Step 3

[]

Fill in and submit the online application

Complete and submit the online application form during the application period. Make sure to upload all required application documents in the application portal according to the "Compulsory Application Documents" list, and check the “Country Specific Documents" section in case anything extra is needed.

Important note for self-funded students: If you apply as self-funded, you are required to upload documentation of financing according to the Norwegian Visa regulations.

If you experience trouble with your application once in the portal, contact [email protected].

-   ●Choose "Login International Applicant" and create a user
-   ●Create a new application, find the programme beneath "Master's Degree Programmes" > "Erasmus Mundus Programmes"
-   ●We advice you to rank all 6 second-year specializations (we cannot guarantee that your preferences will be met)
-   ●Upload all the required documents (make sure to mark them with name and type of docuement, i.e. "Motivation Video", "Course Descriptions", "References" etc.
-   ●Ensure your motivation video link is working well
-   ●Indicate the type of scholarships you want to apply for (read more in the "Scholarships and fees" section)
-   ●In case you would like to apply for an additional special needs scholarship, specify this in the comment field.
-   ●If you are self-funded, you must upload documentation of financing

[]

Important: Late or incomplete applications will not be accepted.

Step 4

[]

Submit your language test scores (if required)

If an official English language test result is required from you, submit it according to test-specific instructions.

-   ●All language test results are verified
-   ●Only the language tests listed under Admission requirements are accepted.
-   ●Final deadline: January 18, 2027
-   ●There are no exceptions to this deadline
-   ●If you submit your English test results before the application deadline, upload them through the application portal.
-   ●If you submit your English test results after the application deadline, but before the final language test deadline, upload your test result through Søknadsweb and clearly mark the document “English Test Results”.
-   ●After uploading your test result in Søknadsweb, you must also send a confirmation email to [email protected]. The email subject must include your application number and “English Test Results”.
-   ●Do not submit English test results by email. The email is only used to confirm that the result has been uploaded in Søknadsweb.

Step 5

[]

Monitor your e-mail for our response

After submitting your application, CYBERSURE Admissions Office will check it and send you an update.

-   ●Submitted applications are checked in order of arrival
-   ●CYBERSURE Admission Office at NTNU will send you an e-mail
-   ●You may be asked to complement your application if required
-   ●Keep your email address up to date

Step 6

[]

If your application is eligible, wait for the selection results

The academic staff evaluates eligible applications and makes the final selection and scholarship decisions.

-   ●Academic evaluation takes place in January-February 2027
-   ●Selection results will be published in mid-March 2027
-   ●All eligible applicants will be informed by e-mail
-   ●Admission letters and practical instructions will be sent to selected students
-   ●Rectification request option available within 14 days after results publication

Student Selection to the Second-Year University

1

Indicate Your Preferences

For the second-year specialization, applicants should indicate their preferences in the online application form. You will rank all six partner universities based on your interests and career goals. In addition, your preferences should be stated in the motivation. We cannot guarantee that you will receive an offer with your highest ranked preferences.

2

Admission to Both Years

The student will be admitted to both the first and the second year during the initial admission phase. Your provisional second-year university assignment will be included in your admission letter.

3

Consortium Selection Decision

The CYBERSURE Consortium decides on the selection to the second-year specializations. The selection is based on:

-   •The student's preferences
-   •The available seats at each university
-   •The suitability of the student to the chosen specialization

4

Guaranteed Assignment

The Consortium partner universities ensure that all eligible selected students will be designated a second-year university. Every admitted student is guaranteed a placement.
