Poland My First Choice NAWA

[Poland My First Choice 1 1]

The Poland My First Choice NAWA programme aims to encourage young talented people from the countries listed below to pursue studies at the Polish university-type higher education institutions (HEIs). The Programme offers a scholarship during the statutory period of full-time second-cycle studies at academic HEIs (both public and non-public) supervised by the Minister of Science and Higher Education. In addition, the Programme provides an exemption from tuition fees at public universities.

Applicants select the university and the field of study at which they want to study independently, based on the offer of universities listed in the call for applications. NAWA does not act as an intermediary in the admission of Applicants for studies. Applicants participate in the admission process at the university of their choice by themselves and independently of the application submitted to the NAWA. Information on studying in Poland can be found at www.study.gov.pl.

The scholarship offer is addressed to foreigners – the citizens of the following countries and territories:

  ---------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ --------------------------
  Australia        Finland                                                                            Japan                                                                          Republic of Korea
  Austria          France                                                                             Latvia                                                                         Romania
  Belgium          Germany                                                                            Liechtenstein                                                                  Singapore
  Bulgaria         Great Britain                                                                      Lithuania                                                                      Slovakia
  Canada           Greece                                                                             Luxembourg                                                                     Slovenia
  Chile            Hong Kong, special administrative region (SAR) of the People's Republic of China   Macau, special administrative region (SAR) of the People's Republic of China   Spain
  Croatia          Hungary                                                                            Malta                                                                          Sweden
  Cyprus           Iceland                                                                            Netherlands                                                                    Switzerland
  Czech Republic   Ireland                                                                            New Zealand                                                                    Taiwan
  Denmark          Israel                                                                             Norway                                                                         United States of America
  Estonia          Italy                                                                              Portugal                                                                       Uruguay
  ---------------- ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ --------------------------

The Programme does not provide for a call for applications for fields of study offered by the HEIs supervised by other ministries.
