FAQ – Swedish Institute Scholarships

The statistics show that most of our successful applicants have carefully read through all the information about our calls for application as well as our FAQ, before submitting their applications. Please make sure that you do the same to get closer to a successful application.

University Admissions and master’s programmes

Do you have any questions related to University Admissions, the application fee or your application number? Please contact University Admissions directly. The Swedish Institute and University Admissions are two separate agencies and SI only administers the SI scholarship.

Application for University Admissions

-   Can I apply for a master’s programme at the Swedish Institute? []
    No. The Swedish Institute (SI) is a government agency, not a university. We administer the SI scholarships, but not university programmes/courses. Please visit University Admissions or contact the Swedish universities directly.
-   Can I apply for any master’s programme and get an SI Scholarship? []
    No. The list of master’s programmes eligible for our scholarship programmes will be available on 13 November 2026 under the respective programme pages: Scholarship for Global Professionals and Pioneering Women in STEM.
-   How do I apply for a master’s programme? []
    You should apply through University Admissions portal. Note that Stockholm School of Economics (SSE) and Konstfack – University of Arts, Crafts and Design have their own separate processes for intake and admissions.
-   I have a problem with my application at University Admissions. Do I need to inform SI? []
    No, you do not need to inform us about any problem you have regarding your application at University Admissions. However, SI will only consider you for our scholarships if you are admitted to one of the eligible master’s programmes by 1 April 2027. We are unfortunately unable to make any exceptions.
-   I changed the ranking of the master’s programmes I applied for. Will it affect my scholarship application? []
    You can find the information on how your application to University Admissions will be affected when you change the ranking here. You don’t need to inform us about your new ranking or any related communication with University Admissions.

    If your application to University Admissions is evaluated at a later time due to the change, it’s unlikely that you will be admitted by 1 April 2027. In that case, your scholarship application will not be considered by SI. If the change in ranking does not delay your admission and you are admitted on time, we will evaluate your scholarship application.
-   I missed the deadline to apply to University Admissions. Can I still apply for the scholarship? []
    Applicants must apply for a master’s programme before applying for our scholarships. The application period at University Admissions is between 16 October and 15 January. If you submit a late application to University Admissions, you may still send in your scholarship application in our application portal. However, there is a risk that your University Admissions application will be given the status “late application”, meaning you might not be admitted in time to be considered for our scholarships. Only applicants who are admitted to one of the eligible master’s programmes on 1 April 2027 will be considered for our scholarships.

Application fee

-   Why do I have to pay an application fee? []
    We do not charge any application fee to apply for our scholarships. There is, however, an application fee that you need to pay when you apply for programmes via University Admissions. If you do not pay the application fee in time, your application to University Admissions will not be processed, and therefore, you cannot be considered for the scholarship. Please contact the University Admissions for questions related to their application fee.
-   How do I pay the application fee? []
    For questions regarding the admissions process to the University Admissions and their application fee, please contact University Admissions directly.
-   Is the application fee refundable? []
    No, it is not refundable. Note that the application fee is not related to your scholarship application.
-   Do I need to inform SI after I pay the application fee? []
    No, you do not need to inform us.
-   I have only applied to the Stockholm School of Economics or Konstfack. Do I have to pay the application fee? []
    The Stockholm School of Economics (SSE) and Konstfack have their own application processes. Please read through the application process thoroughly and contact the universities directly should you have any questions:

    Stockholm School of Economics
    Konstfack – University of Arts, Crafts and Design

Application number

-   How do I know what my application number is? []
    Once you have submitted your application for master’s programmes to University Admissions, you will get a personal application number consisting of seven or eight digits. Log in to “My pages” at universityadmissions.se, to find the number. You will need the application number when you apply for an SI Scholarship.

    If you applied to the Stockholm School of Economics (SSE) or applied for any other eligible master’s programmes that are not handled by University Admissions, you should enter seven or eight zeroes (i.e. 00000000) as application number in your scholarship application.

Required documents for University Admissions

-   What documents do I need to submit? []
    The Swedish Institute and University Admissions are two separate government agencies. You should contact University Admissions or the university directly if you have questions regarding the documents required for your application to them. If you fail to submit the required documents to University Admissions on time, your application may be deemed late, and then you will not be considered for our scholarships. Only the applicants who are admitted to an eligible master’s programme by 1 April 2027 will be considered for our scholarships.
-   The result of my English test was sent to University Admissions too late. What should I do? []
    You do not need to inform us about it. Instead you should contact University Admissions or the Swedish universities you are applying to. If you cannot be admitted to any eligible master’s programme by 1 April 2027 due to this late submission, your scholarship application will not be considered. We cannot make any exceptions.

SI Scholarships

Who can apply?

-   How do I know if there is any scholarship I can apply for? []
    SI is currently calling for applications for two scholarships: SI Scholarships for Global Professionals and Pioneering Women in STEM. Please read more on their websites to know what the eligible master’s programmes are and what eligibility criteria are required.
-   Can I submit an application for both SI Scholarship for Global Professionals and SI Scholarship Pioneering Women in STEM at the same time? []
    You can only submit one application in our portal and you can only be awarded one scholarships. if you qualify for Pioneering Women in STEM you will automatically be considered for that scholarship. 
-   I currently live in a country not eligible for the SI scholarships. May I still apply? []
    Yes, as long as you hold a citizenship in one of the eligible countries, you are eligible to apply for the SI scholarship. We do not take into consideration the country in which you are currently residing when making our selection.
-   I have a dual citizenship. Which one should I use in my scholarship application? []
    If you have dual citizenship(s) and both are eligible for the scholarship you are applying for, you should only choose one citizenship when submitting your application to SI. Applicants who apply more than once using different citizenships, will have both applications disqualified.

    Applicants whose dual citizenship is in a country not listed on the DAC list of ODA recipients are not eligible for our scholarships.
-   Are there any SI scholarships for bachelor’s studies? []
    No, there are currently no scholarships available from SI for bachelor level studies.
-   Are there any SI scholarships for single courses, exchange studies, study visits or participation in conferences? []
    No. Here you can find all opportunitites for funding and grants currently offered by SI.
-   Do you provide scholarships for PhD studies and postdoctoral research in Sweden? []
    No, there are currently no SI scholarships available for PhD studies or postdoc research in Sweden.

    Applying for PhD and postdoc research programmes works differently in Sweden, read more about it here. 
-   Is my work or leadership experience valid? []
    We require our scholars to have both work and leadership experience. You can find the detailed information under the respective programme pages: Scholarship for Global Professionals and Pioneering Women in STEM

    We are unfortunately not able to comment or assist on individual applications beforehand, as this would create an unfair advantage. Please use your best judgment and decide what you believe are the most appropriate experiences to include in your application. If you believe your experiences should be counted as work/leadership experience, you can count them, as long as you can provide the required proof/validation of these experiences.

    Experiences that cannot be validated according to our requirements will not be considered by the selection committee. Additionally, the competition for our scholarships is high, and the selection committee will prioritise those who have a strong professional background and documented leadership experience from employment within certain fields. Read more about the priorities on our respective programme pages.
-   I am currently enrolled in a master's programme in Sweden. Can I apply for a scholarship for my second year? []
    No. If you are already enrolled in a master’s programme in Sweden you are not eligible to apply. We suggest that you look for other scholarship opportunities.
-   What requirements do you have for English proficiency and GPA? []
    SI does not have any requirements for language proficiency and GPA for applicants on master’s level, as these assessments are done by the universities and University Admissions.

    Find more information regarding language proficiency and potential GPA requirements here or on the specific university’s website.
-   I have applied for other scholarships. Can I still apply for SI scholarships? []
    Yes. The competition for SI scholarships is high. We recommend that you also look for and apply for other scholarships from your government, organisations in your country or from the Swedish universities where you would like to study.
-   Why do applicants have to be liable to pay tuition fees? []
    To be eligible for the scholarship, you must be liable to pay tuition fees to Swedish universities in accordance with the information provided by University Admissions.

    This does not mean that you need to pay tuition fees by your own means if you are awarded one of our scholarships. It only means you need to be required to pay when you apply. In general, citizens of countries that are not part of the European Union (EU), European Economic Area (EEA) or Switzerland are required to pay tuition fees. Our scholarships cover both tuition fees and living expenses, which means EU citizens (for example) are not the target group of our scholarships.

    The tuition fee will be paid by SI directly to the university in case you are awarded one of our scholarships.

    Kindly contact University Admissions directly if you have questions about whether or not you need to pay tuition fees.
-   Are there other scholarships available for studies in Sweden? I was planning on applying to SI SGP, but now I can’t since my country isn’t on the list. []
    We recommend that you apply for other scholarships provided by the Swedish universities where you would like to study and other organisations, have a look at our list at Study in Sweden. 

     
-   How do you select the eligible countries and how come my country is no longer eligible? []
    The selection of eligible countries is guided by the government strategy that the Swedish Institute reports to. SI has received new directives for our regulatory mandate which has led to new priorities for the activities that the Swedish Institute offers. We fully understand that the changes are disappointing for students from the affected countries.

    If your country is no longer eligible for our scholarships, we recommend that you look for other scholarships from your government, organisations in your country or from the Swedish universities where you would like to study.   

     
-   I am a current scholarship holder. My country is no longer available, will this affect me? []
    No, all current scholars will receive their scholarship as planned. The changes only affect new aspiring scholars.  
-   Can non-binary people apply for Pioneering Women in STEM? []
    No. The scholarship is currently open only to applicants who are legally recognised as women in their official identification documents, such as a passport or national ID. Eligibility is based on the legal gender marker in these documents.

    We respect that gender identity is diverse and personal. However, for the purposes of this scholarship, we must follow the legal gender designation stated in official documents.

When should I apply?

-   When is the scholarship application period? []
    The scholarship application period for master’s studies starting in the autumn semester 2027 is 8-24 February 2027. The application portal will close at 14:59 CET, 24 February 2027.
-   I missed the deadline to apply to University Admissions. Can I still apply for the scholarship? []
    First, you must apply for a master’s programme, and then you can apply for our scholarships. The application period at University Admissions is from October to January. If you send in your application late, you may still submit your scholarship application in our application portal. However, the risk is high that your application to the universities will be evaluated as a “late application”, which can delay admission. Only applicants who are admitted to one of the eligible master’s programmes by 1 April 2027 will be considered for our scholarships. We suggest that you also look for other scholarship opportunities.
-   Do you accept late applications for SI scholarships? []
    Unfortunately, we do not have the possibility to accept late applications. The scholarship application period for master’s studies starting in the autumn semester 2027 is 8-24 February 2027. If you missed the deadline, we suggest you look for other scholarship opportunities.

How to apply?

-   How do I submit my application? []
    We only accept digital applications submitted via our online application portal. You can find a link to the online application portal in the call for applications, during the application period only. It is important to read through all the information on our website as well as the instructions in the forms before applying.

Required documents

-   What documents are required for the application for the SI Scholarships? []
    Find information regarding the document needed for your scholarship application on the respective programme pages under the section ‘How to apply’: Scholarship for Global Professionals and Pioneering Women in STEM.

    There you will also find the mandatory SI templates to use when submitting your documents. Any document that is not requested by SI will not be considered.
-   Can I submit my documents in a different format? []
    You must use the mandatory SI templates uploaded on the programme pages. If any mandatory document is not in the format as required, the application will be deemed ineligible. We make no exceptions.

    Note! The SI forms are updated each year, and thus forms downloaded from previous years are not valid.

Required document - Reference letters

-   From where should I get the letters of reference? []
    We require two letters of reference. At least one letter of reference must be based on your work experience (including both part-time and full-time employment). The second letter of reference can be based on your academic performance or any type of work experience. However, it will be a merit if this second letter of reference is based on your involvement in networks or civil society organisations outside of your regular work. If you are not able to provide these documents, your application will be deemed ineligible. We make no exceptions.
-   Can my referee fill out the form in my native language and then translate it? []
    We accept translations as long as they are performed by a notarised translator with the notary office’s official stamp. It is not acceptable for individual applicants to translate the documents on their own. The original document and the translation should be uploaded as one PDF.

    You can find more information in the instruction in the form.
-   Can you contact me before you contact my referees? []
    Due to the large amount of applications, we are unfortunately unable to inform any applicants in advance. You should try to ensure that the contact details of the referees that you provide are valid and remain unchanged at least until 22 April 2027. You should also ensure that the referees check their email, including their spam-box regularly during this period and answer our emails in time.
-   There are fields called "Choose one" but I don't see any options to choose from. []
    Click the text “Choose one” and you will see an arrow to the right. Click the arrow and a drop-down list will be shown. Choose the one option that suits you the best.

    If you are using a Mac-computer and are unable to click at the drop-down lists, we suggest that you try filling out the form on a PC instead.

Required document – Proof of Work and Leadership Experience

-   How do I calculate my employment hours? []
    Here are two examples of how to calculate your employment hours. 

    Scenario 1:
    The applicant is currently employed full-time, working 40 hours per week for 50 weeks per year for 1 calendar year prior to 8 February 2027, and previously worked part-time as freelancer 12 hours per week for 28 weeks per year for 3 calendar years. 

    The calculation is (40 hours x 50 weeks x 1 year = 2,000) + (12 hours x 28 weeks x 3 years = 1,008) = 3,008 hours. 

    An applicant within this scenario meets the eligibility criteria for the work experience required for our scholarships

      

    Scenario 2:
    The applicant owns a company as self-employed, working 10 hours per week for 35 weeks per year for 3 calendar years. The company will close down on 31 December 2026 and the applicant does not have another employment. 

    The calculation is (10 hours x 35 weeks x 3 years) = 1,050 hours. 

    An applicant within this scenario fails to meet the eligibility criteria for a scholarship due to the lack of work experience hours. 
-   Can you count internships towards the hours of work experience needed to apply for the SI scholarship? []
    Although internships are great experiences, we do not consider them employment. Valid work experience includes full-time or part-time employment, freelancing or entrepreneurship.
-   Who can sign the Proof of Work and Leadership Experience form? []
    The person who signs for your work and leadership experience should be someone who knows you in a professional rather than a personal capacity.

    -   It should be someone who knows about your experience at the company/organisation. It does not have to be your direct manager. It can be, for example, the HR manager or the head of the organisation, as long as he/she can comment on your experience on behalf of your employer.
    -   If you work in a student organisation at your university, you may ask a staff at the university who knows about your experience to certify it and it can be stamped by either the university or the organisation.
    -   If you work as a freelancer, you may ask the professional association or agency, where you are registered as a freelancer or receive work from, to sign and stamp.
    -   If you work in a family business or you are the owner, we can accept a signature from a client or a staff that works at your company but who is not a family member. However, the client should not be a private person but rather a company/organisation. The client is only allowed to certify the working hours that you have worked for them. The stamp should be the official stamp of the company/organisation.

    Applicants must use our form uploaded on the programme pages. If you are not able to provide the required proof, your application will be deemed ineligible. We make no exceptions.
-   Does my employer(s) need to stamp my proof of work and leadership experience form? []
    Yes, all applicants need to prove the authenticity of the required documents for his/her applications.

    If you are not able to get a traditional stamp for your reference letter you could explore the possibility to sign the document with a digital signature or stamp. As our scholarships are offered to a variety of nationalities, we are not able to assist with further information regarding how to proceed, as all countries have their own policies and standards.
-   Applicants should only submit one proof of work and leadership experience, but I have worked at more than one workplace. []
    You must use the correct SI template for Proof of Work and Leadership Experience to demonstrate your work and leadership experience. The form consists of one part for work experience and one part for leadership experience.

    You can submit up to three work experience forms to demonstrate up to three different work experiences; you can submit up to two leadership experience forms to demonstrate up to two different leadership experiences.

    You should merge all the forms into one PDF-file. All the work experience forms should be presented first in reverse chronological order, and then followed by all leadership experience forms sorted in reverse chronological order.

    Each one of the forms should not exceed one page and in total the entire PDF-file submitted should not exceed five pages (except the instruction page).

    You can find more information in the instructions in the respective form.
-   Do I have to provide proof for all the work experience I have had so far? []
    No, you do not have to provide proof for each of your work experiences. However you do have to follow these requirements.

    Work experience requirements:

    Applicants from – Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Moldova, Ukraine  – must be able to provide proof of work experience. There is no requirement of number of hours, however applicants with more experience will be prioritised.

    -   If you cannot provide proof of work experience your application will be deemed ineligible.

    Applicants from – Bangladesh, Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Ecuador, Egypt, Ethiopia, Guatemala, Indonesia, Iraq, Jordan, Kenya, Liberia, Malaysia, Mexico, Morocco, Nigeria, Peru, Philippines, Rwanda, Senegal, South Africa, Tanzania, Thailand, Uganda, Uzbekistan, Vietnam, Zambia – must have at least 3,000 hours of work experience prior to 8 February 2027. You do not have to meet the requirement within one period of employment. The 3,000 hours must have been accumulated from a maximum of three employers. 

    -   If you cannot provide proof for a total of 3,000 hours of work experience or more, then your application will be deemed ineligible.
    -   If three experiences are not enough for you to meet the requirement for 3,000 hours, your application will be deemed ineligible.

    You can find more information in the instructions in the respective forms.
-   I have a special situation so I can’t provide the required proof of work and leadership experience. What should I do? []
    If you want to be considered for our scholarships, you must provide the required documents. We make no exceptions. If you are not able to follow our instructions, your application will be deemed ineligible.

    You are of course welcome to apply again next year.
-   Can you contact me before you contact my referees? []
    Due to the large amount of applications, we are unfortunately unable to inform any applicants in advance. You should try to ensure that the contact details of the referees that you provide are valid and remain unchanged at least until the day of the announcment of the awarded scholarships. You should also ensure that the referees check their email, including their spam-box regularly during this period and answer our emails in time.
-   There are fields called "Choose one" but I don't see any options to choose from. []
    Click the text “Choose one” and you will see an arrow to the right. Click the arrow and a drop-down list will be shown. Choose the one option that suits you the best.

    If you are using a Mac computer and are unable to click at the drop-down lists, we suggest that you try filling out the form on a PC instead.

Required document – CV

-   Should I attach any annexes in my CV, such as documents proving the outcome of my work? []
    No, any additional pages will be disregarded.

    It is up to you to decide which parts in the CV form are relevant for your application, and you can delete the parts that are not relevant for you. The maximum length of the CV is three pages.

    You must use the mandatory SI template for CV that is available on the respective programme pages. An application with a CV in any other format (including Europass CV) will be disqualified.
-   There are fields called "Choose one" but I don't see any options to choose from. []
    Click the text “Choose one” and you will see an arrow to the right. Click the arrow and a drop-down list will be shown. Choose the one option that suits you the best.

    If you are using a Mac computer and are unable to click at the drop-down lists, we suggest that you try filling out the form on a PC instead.

Scholarship period

-   Is it possible to defer the scholarship period or take a study leave? []
    Deferral of an SI scholarship can only be granted once you have started your studies.
-   Is there any type of insurance included in the scholarship? []
    Our scholarships do not include insurance coverage. We recommend that you turn to the university you are applying to, to receive more information about their specific arrangements regarding insurance for international students.
