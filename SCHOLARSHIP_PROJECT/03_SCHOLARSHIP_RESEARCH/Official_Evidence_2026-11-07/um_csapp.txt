Applying for graduate programs in Computer Science

With the guidelines on this page, you can streamline your application process and increase your chances of successfully pursuing a graduate degree in Computer Science.

Application process

The application process for graduate programs in Computer Science typically spans several months. During this time, applicants must complete various stages, such as gathering necessary documents, obtaining recommendation letters and fulfilling admission requirements

The minimum entry requirements for each program are detailed in the graduate handbook (policies and forms) and the University Calendar. The Faculty of Graduate Studies maintains an international degree equivalency and grade-point conversions page.

Before applying

Before applying to the MSc or PhD program in Computer Science, securing personal acceptance from a faculty member to act as your supervisor is a prerequisite. It is essential to establish this mentor-student relationship before commencing the application process to enhance your chances of admission and academic success.

-   [a student programming a robot]

-   Please explore the research areas for active research groups and directly email potential supervisors. Information about individual faculty members’ interests can also be found by clicking on their names on the faculty and staff page.

    Only professors can be supervisors; instructors and adjunct professors are not eligible. Applications without an endorsement from a faculty member will not be considered by the department admissions committee. 

    You must have a confirmed supervisor before you can be considered for admission.

When contacting prospective supervisors, please ensure that you are matching your research interest to their area of study. Email them with a short introduction and unofficial copies of your transcripts, degree certificate if awarded, English language score if required and curriculum vitae. As faculty members receive many requests, they may only be able to reply to those applicants they are considering supervising.

Applying

If you have a supervisor who is supporting your application, please apply online through the Faculty of Graduate Studies. Supervisor support does not guarantee admission to a program.

Applying online through the Faculty of Graduate Studies

Applications to our programs are competitive. Meeting the minimum requirements (detailed in the graduate handbook, policies, and forms) does not guarantee admission. The decision to determine if an applicant has the required background and recommend admission to the Faculty of Graduate Studies rests with the Department of Computer Science.
