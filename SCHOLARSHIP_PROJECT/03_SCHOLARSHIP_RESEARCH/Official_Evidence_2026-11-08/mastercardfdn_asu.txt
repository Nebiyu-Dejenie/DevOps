Arizona State University (ASU)
About
Arizona State University is an institution committed to excellence, access, and impact. It pursues research that contributes to the public good.
The Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at ASU provides access to quality education to high-achieving African students who face significant economic barriers and is currently in its Phase II with a special focus on Strengthening Institutional Linkages.
