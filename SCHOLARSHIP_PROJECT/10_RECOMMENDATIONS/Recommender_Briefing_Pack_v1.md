# Recommender Briefing Pack v1.0

**Purpose:** give each referee accurate facts and deadlines, so that **they write their own letter**. Do not send a pre-written letter for them to sign. CMU-Africa, Erasmus Mundus and SI collect references directly from referees, and SI uses its own template that must be signed and stamped.

## What to send each referee (by 15 Nov 2026)

1. **A short request message** (below), personalised.
2. **Your verified one-page summary:** degree, CGPA 2.62, final-year averages, final project, your role with them.
3. **Programme list and deadlines:**
   - CMU-Africa: early 15 Dec 2026
   - Sweden SI: reference letters on the SI 2027/28 template (available after 13 Nov), needed by early Feb 2027
   - Erasmus Mundus: per call
4. **What you hope they can speak to** (only things they actually observed).

## Request message (template; edit before sending)

> Dear [Title Name],
>
> I am applying for fully funded master's programmes for 2027, including [Carnegie Mellon University Africa's MS in Information Technology] and [the Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals]. Would you be willing to act as one of my referees?
>
> You would receive an email link from the university to submit a confidential reference. The first deadline is [15 December 2026].
>
> To help, I attach a one-page summary of my studies and work. If you agree, I would be grateful if your letter could mention what you observed of [my final-year project / my work on X at SantimPay]. Please only include what you can personally confirm.
>
> Thank you very much for considering this.
>
> [Name]

## Referee-specific points (facts only)

| Referee | They can truthfully address | They should NOT be asked to claim |
|---|---|---|
| Final-project supervisor | Project topic, your part, grade (A−), technical ability | Rankings they do not know; overall academic excellence |
| Lecturer (A− course) | Performance in that course (Compiler Design / Advanced Programming / Complexity Theory) | "Top student" unless true |
| SantimPay manager | Your actual responsibilities, a concrete contribution, reliability, any leadership | Metrics they cannot confirm |
| Kifiya supervisor | Security and IT operations work you did | Dates or titles different from HR records |
