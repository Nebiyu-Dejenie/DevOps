# 5. Interview programme

## 5.1 Format

From week 3, we run **one mock interview a week** in this session:

- one question at a time
- you type or dictate your answer
- I correct grammar, vocabulary, clarity, structure, relevance and confidence markers, then give one improved version **built only from your verified facts**

## 5.2 Answer method

**PEER: Point → Example → Effect → Reflection.** 60–90 seconds per answer.

## 5.3 Core question bank

### A. GPA and academic weaknesses (hardest; prepare first)

1. Your GPA is 2.62. Why should we admit you?
2. Why did you fail Probability & Statistics and Discrete Mathematics on the first attempt?
3. Our programme is mathematically demanding. How will you cope?
4. What does your extension-programme study mode mean?
5. Which course did you find hardest, and what did you learn from it?
6. Tell us about your final-year project.

**Model answer framework for Q1** (facts verified; delivery is yours):

> "My cumulative GPA is 2.62, and I won't hide that my first years were weak, especially in mathematics. But the trend matters: my final-year semester averages were 3.05 and 3.35, and my best grades were in Compiler Design, Advanced Programming, Complexity Theory and my final-year project, all A−. Since graduating I have worked [⏳ N] years on production payment infrastructure, which has given me a practical foundation that many graduates don't have. I know mathematics is where I must work hardest, and I'm [⏳ real preparation step]."

### B. Professional experience (answers wait for the HR-confirmed facts)

7. Walk us through your current role.
8. Describe a production incident you handled. What was the root cause?
9. How does your CI/CD pipeline work, step by step?
10. What did you automate with Ansible, and why?
11. How do you monitor services? What alert would wake you at 3 a.m.?
12. How do you secure access to production systems?
13. Explain how a POS payment travels from the terminal to the bank. *(Answer only to the depth you actually know.)*
14. You worked at SantimPay and Kifiya at the same time. How did that work? *(**Must match the HR letters exactly.**)*
15. What is the biggest mistake you have made at work?

### C. Leadership

16. Describe a time you led people without formal authority.
17. Tell us about a conflict in a team and how it was resolved.
18. How did you help someone else learn a skill?
19. What does leadership mean in a technical team?
20. Describe a decision you made with incomplete information.

### D. Motivation and fit

21. Why this programme? Why this university or country?
22. Why now, after [⏳ N] years of work?
23. What will you do if you don't receive the scholarship?
24. What will you contribute to your classmates?

### E. Ethiopia, Africa and the future

25. How will you use this degree in Ethiopia?
26. What is the biggest technology challenge in Ethiopian digital payments?
27. Where do you see yourself 5 and 10 years after graduating?
28. How will you give back to your community? *(Real commitments only.)*
29. Would you return to Ethiopia? Why? *(Return obligations: Mastercard Foundation, Chevening, Fulbright J-1.)*
30. What would you change about Ethiopia's tech ecosystem?

### F. Technical and academic (CS fundamentals; revise these)

31. Explain CAP theorem trade-offs in a payment system.
32. What happens when you type a URL into a browser?
33. Process vs thread; container vs virtual machine.
34. How does TLS establish a secure connection?
35. What is the principle of least privilege? Give an example from your work.
36. How does Kubernetes restart a failed pod?
37. Explain public-key cryptography to a non-expert.
38. What is idempotency, and why does it matter in Ansible and in payments?
39. Big-O of common operations on a hash map vs a balanced tree.
40. How would you design a highly available service on two datacentres?

The full bank (50 scholarship, 30 technical, 20 leadership, 20 experience questions) with model answers will be completed **after** the experience facts are verified, because answers cannot be modelled on unconfirmed roles.
