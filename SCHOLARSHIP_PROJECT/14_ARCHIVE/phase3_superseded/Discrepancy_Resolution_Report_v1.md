# 1. Purpose and rules

This report lists every conflict found across your documents and says, for each one:

- which source carries the most weight
- what evidence would settle it
- what only you can confirm
- the information we will use, either verified now or pending your confirmation

**Rules applied:**

1. I never pick the version that looks better. Where documents conflict, the field stays **PENDING** until primary evidence (registrar or HR document) or your written confirmation settles it.
2. **Authority ranking** (strongest first):
   1. Official institutional document (registrar transcript or certificate; signed HR experience letter, contract or payslip)
   2. Third-party verification (certification verification page, official correspondence)
   3. Your written confirmation
   4. Your own CVs and LinkedIn, which are self-declared and lowest
3. **Status labels:**
   - **RESOLVED (document):** settled by an institutional document
   - **RESOLVED (calculation):** settled by arithmetic on an institutional document
   - **PENDING (evidence)**
   - **PENDING (your confirmation)**
   - **EXCLUDE:** not to be used in any application unless new evidence appears
