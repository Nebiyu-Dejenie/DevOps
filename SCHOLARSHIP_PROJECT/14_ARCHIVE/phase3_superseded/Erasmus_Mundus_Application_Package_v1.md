# 1. Rules that apply to all Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (E2: erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu)

- Each consortium sets its own admission rules, deadline and selection. You apply **directly** to the consortium.
- Applications usually open October to January for a September start.
- You may apply to **at most 3** Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters per cycle (reported by secondary sources; confirm in each call).
- A person who has already held an Erasmus Mundus scholarship cannot receive another.

# 5. Motivation letter skeleton (CYBERSURE; tailored version after the facts are confirmed)

**Structure (about 1 page; check the call for the limit):**

1. **Opening, the problem you work on (≈ 80 words):** securing infrastructure that carries payments in Ethiopia. Concrete context: POS, QR and payment-gateway services delivered with partner banks.
2. **What you have done (≈ 150 words):** ⏳ confirmed security work, e.g. IAM/MFA administration, WAF/WAAP and PAM/DAM support at Kifiya (dates per HR), access controls and monitoring at SantimPay. One concrete example.
3. **Academic foundation and honest record (≈ 100 words):**
   - Computer Security (B), Distributed Systems (B−), Compiler Design (A−), Complexity Theory (A−)
   - CGPA 2.62 with final-year 3.05 / 3.35, stated plainly
   - Mathematics as a known weakness, and how you are addressing it
4. **Why CYBERSURE (≈ 150 words):** ⏳ name specific modules or tracks from the official curriculum. Why "assurance" (verification, risk, compliance) fits financial services. Why the mobility path (NTNU first year, then a second-year partner) suits you.
5. **Goal and return (≈ 80 words):** security engineering for Ethiopian and African fintech and banks. Concrete, modest, five-year goal.

**What not to write:**

- "Passionate since childhood"
- "Excelled academically"
- Tools you cannot discuss
- Any generic paragraph copied from the 2024 Italy letter
