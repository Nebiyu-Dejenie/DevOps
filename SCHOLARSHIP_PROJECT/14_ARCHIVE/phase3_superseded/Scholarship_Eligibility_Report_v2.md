# 2. Alternative pathways for a 2.62 CGPA

**Principle:** work experience **does not replace** a mandatory academic minimum anywhere in this list. It adds weight where admission is holistic, or where the scholarship explicitly scores professional experience.

| Pathway | Where it applies (verified) | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **No GPA cut-off, holistic review** | CMU-Africa (E2) | Strong essays, technical test, references, English score |
| **Scholarship that explicitly scores work and leadership** | SI SISGP (E2); Chevening 2028 (E2) | Employer-signed proof of hours and leadership |
| **Entrance exam or interview outweighs grades** | Stipendium Hungaricum: Szeged scoring, ELTE entry test (E2) | Prepare for programming and maths entrance tests |
| **Final-years GPA instead of cumulative** | University of Manitoba (last 2 years) (E2) | Registrar certification of later-year grades |
| **Research master's with a funded supervisor** | Canadian thesis MSc (Manitoba; Memorial, where admission follows a supervisor's funding offer) (E2 / E3) | Email supervisors with a research interest that matches your operations background |
| **University-specific CSC (Type B)** | Chinese universities set their own bar (E2) | Ask target universities for a pre-admission review |

## Documents that could genuinely help

1. **Registrar class-rank / percentile letter.** It helps only if it is favourable (GKS top 20%; EPOS upper third). If the rank is unfavourable, do not submit it where it is optional.
2. **The grading-scale legend already on your transcript.** It describes C+ and B− as "Good". Your CGPA of 2.62 sits in that band, and you may cite this factually: "2.62 on a scale where 2.5–2.75 is designated 'Good'".
3. **Certified last-two-years GPA.** Useful for Manitoba and any "final years" reviewer.
4. **A short academic explanation** (only where a form invites it, ≤ 150 words). Cover the extension mode, the repeated maths courses, and the final-year 3.05 / 3.35. No hardship you cannot document.
