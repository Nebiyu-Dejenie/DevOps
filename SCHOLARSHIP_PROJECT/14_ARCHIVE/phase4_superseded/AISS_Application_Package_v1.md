# AISS (Erasmus Mundus): Application Package v1

**Programme:** Artificial Intelligence for Sustainable Societies. Tallinn University (Estonia), Lusófona University (Portugal), Tampere University (Finland). Two years, three countries.

**Application window:** **2 Dec 2026 – 2 Feb 2027**, via Tallinn University's DreamApply. Verified on aissprogram.eu, 7 Nov 2026 (E1).

## 1. Why this programme suits your profile (verified selection method)

- Eligibility: a recognised bachelor's plus "an academic, **professional or project** background that can support your proposed AISS study path" (E1).
- Selection: **documents 50%** (motivation video, portfolio, other documents) + **interview 50%**. Minimum positive result: 35 (E1).
- **GPA is not a published scoring criterion.** The education documents are checked for eligibility. Your professional portfolio and interview carry the weight.
- Scholarship: **€1,400/month** (covers insurance, mobility travel, housing and food). At most 10% of scholarships go to one nationality (E1).
- English: IELTS Academic **or IELTS Online** 6.5, with at least 5.5 in every component (E1).

**My assessment:** this is one of your best realistic chances among Erasmus Mundus programmes. The programme is about AI *for society*, so present yourself honestly: an infrastructure engineer who wants the AI and design knowledge to build digital public services (such as payments) that are reliable, fair and sustainable. Do not present yourself as an AI researcher.

## 2. Documents checklist (E1)

- [ ] Education documents and transcript, in English. A certified translation is not needed, because your documents are issued in English.
- [ ] Valid English evidence (IELTS 6.5 / ≥ 5.5 each).
- [ ] **Motivation video, max 1 min 30 s.** Uploaded via DreamApply as a link, not on social media. You must be on screen at least half the time, using your own voice only (no AI voice). Do not speed up the video.
- [ ] **Portfolio: up to 5 executed projects, each with a brief description.** Images, presentations, applications and GitHub links are allowed. It must be a summary, not a list of links.
- [ ] **Self-funding plan (mandatory, or the application is rejected).** A short document on how you would cover tuition, living costs and travel if not awarded a scholarship.
- [ ] Passport copy. A sworn affidavit is needed if your name differs across documents.
- [ ] Apply personally. Applications by agents are not accepted.

## 5. Self-funding plan (honest structure)

AISS rejects applications without this plan. Fill it with **real** figures only. Do not overstate savings or sponsors.

1. **Expected costs:** take tuition and living-cost figures from AISS's Participation Costs and Living Costs pages (check them on the live site; they did not render in this session).
2. **Your realistic sources:** personal savings [amount], family contribution [amount, if any], employer support [only if formally offered], other scholarships applied for (Sweden SI, EDISS, Hungary).
3. **Plan B:** "If I am not awarded the Erasmus Mundus scholarship and cannot cover the costs, I will [defer / not enrol / apply for the self-funded round only if funding is secured]." An honest Plan B is better than an unrealistic budget.

## 6. Interview (15 min: 10 min your presentation + 5 min questions)

**Required structure (E1):**

1. **Personal introduction** (about 3 min): education, then infrastructure work, then one concrete example of a real problem.
2. **Motivation** (about 4 min): topics you want to explore (e.g. trustworthy AI in digital financial services; participatory design with merchants and users).
3. **Skill set and career** (about 3 min): what you expect to learn, and how it applies in Ethiopia.

**Rules:** be punctual (rescheduling is impossible); do not read from the screen; answer authentically. Practise with me: mock interviews start in week 3 of the plan.
