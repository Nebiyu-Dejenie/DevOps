## 4. Motivation video script (2 minutes, about 260 spoken words)

> [0:00] Hello, my name is [name]. I am a Computer Science graduate from Ethiopia, and I work on the infrastructure behind digital-payment services [⏳ verified role and years].
>
> [0:15] **Why EDISS:** every day I see how much our systems depend on data: transaction logs, monitoring metrics, alerts. But most of my work is reacting to problems. I want to learn how to *engineer* data-intensive software systems properly: how to design them, test them, and use intelligent methods so they fail less and recover faster. EDISS combines exactly these: software engineering, data engineering and intelligent systems.
>
> [0:50] **What I bring:** practical production experience with CI/CD pipelines, automation with Ansible, containers and monitoring [⏳ verified tools only]. My undergraduate record started weak, but I improved strongly. My final year was my best year, with A− grades in Compiler Design, Advanced Programming, Complexity Theory and my final-year project.
>
> [1:25] **Why choose me:** I will bring real operational problems into class discussions and team projects, and I am used to working with colleagues under pressure when systems go down. After EDISS, I want to return to Ethiopia's fintech sector to build reliable, data-driven platforms that people trust with their money.
>
> [1:50] Thank you for considering my application.

**Recording tips:** good light, quiet room, look at the camera, speak slowly. Do not read from the screen. Keep it within 1:30–2:30.
