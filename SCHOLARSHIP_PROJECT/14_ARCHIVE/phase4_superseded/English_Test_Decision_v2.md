## Decision

1. **Take IELTS Academic, in-centre, once**, early to mid December 2026. **Target 7.0 overall, no band below 6.5.** This satisfies EDISS's strict "no band below 6.0" with a margin.
   - Where: British Council, Addis Ababa University (6 Kilo).
   - Fee: **ETB 32,800** (E1, ielts.org).
   - EDISS needs the Test Report Form number at application (deadline 12 Jan 2027).
2. **Take the DET only for CMU-Africa.** CMU-Africa covers the fee for first-time applicants who apply by 15 Dec (E2).
3. **Skip TOEFL** (US$185 in Ethiopia, E1) and **skip PTE**: neither adds a programme on your list.
4. Sweden needs no test, so the SI application is not affected by test timing.
