# Sweden: SI Scholarship for Global Professionals, Application Package v1

All rules below were verified on si.se and universityadmissions.se on 7 Nov 2026 (E1).

**Ethiopia, 2026 round:** 793 applications, **13 scholarships** (1.6%). Your application quality decides.

## 1. Two separate applications

| Step | Where | When | What |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. University application | universityadmissions.se | **16 Oct 2026 – 15 Jan 2027** | Up to **4 SI-eligible master's programmes**, ranked |

## 2. Programme selection method (do this on 13–20 Nov)

1. On **13 Nov**, open SI's list of eligible programmes for 2027/28 (published on the SI page).
2. Filter to STEM programmes matching your direction: **computer systems / cloud / networks / cybersecurity / software engineering**.
3. For each candidate, open the programme page on universityadmissions.se and the university's page. Record:
   - specific entry requirements (credits in maths or programming)
   - **selection basis** (often "academic record, motivation and CV": write down exactly what it says)
   - any extra documents (letter of intent, CV, course descriptions)
4. Choose **4**:
   - **two** where your profile meets the specific requirements comfortably
   - **one** strong-fit programme
   - **one** programme whose selection weighs the CV and motivation, not only grades

   Rank them by **real preference**. SI can only fund the programme you are admitted to on 1 April.
5. I will prepare the comparison table as soon as the list is published.

## 3. SI motivation (portal text): draft structure (about 500 words; final after the HR letters)

SI evaluates "talented professionals with capability, an existing platform and ambition to contribute to the further development of their home country and region" and expects you to link your programme to relevant **sustainability goals** (E1).

> **1. The development problem I work on.** Ethiopia's financial system is moving from cash to digital payments. Merchants, schools and banks depend on platforms that must run reliably and securely. I work on [⏳ verified role] at SantimPay, a fintech whose POS, QR-payment and school-payment services are used through partner banks.
>
> **2. My platform and leadership.** [⏳ One verified leadership example: what you led, how many people, what decisions you made, what changed. SI defines leadership as leading colleagues, allocating tasks, or influencing strategy or decisions.]
>
> **3. Why this programme.** [Programme name] will give me [2 specific course areas from the official curriculum] that I cannot learn on the job: the design of secure, resilient distributed systems. This links to **SDG 9** (resilient infrastructure and innovation) and **SDG 8** (inclusive economic growth): reliable digital payments let small businesses take part in the formal economy.
>
> **4. What I will do after.** Return to Ethiopia's fintech or banking sector to [realistic 3–5-year goal, e.g. lead infrastructure reliability and security for payment platforms], and share practices with colleagues [⏳ only real plans].
>
> **5. Why me.** [⏳ 2–3 sentences: verified experience, the final-year improvement in my studies, and commitment.]

## 5. Proof of Work and Leadership form plan

- Up to **3 work forms** + up to **2 leadership forms**; one page each; **max 5 pages** in one PDF. Order: work forms first, newest first; then leadership forms.
- SI counts **3,000+ hours from at most 3 employers**, accumulated before 8 Feb 2027. **Internships do not count.** Use Employment_Evidence_Collection_Form.xlsx to check.
- Signed by someone who knows your work on behalf of the employer (HR Manager is acceptable) and stamped with the official company stamp, or by a notary.

## 6. References (SI template, signed and stamped)

- **Reference 1 (mandatory, work-based):** your SantimPay manager.
- **Reference 2:** a second work referee, or academic. **A merit if it is based on civil society or network involvement** (only if real).
- Referees must answer SI e-mails until **22 Apr 2027**.

## 7. Integrity note

SI states that all applications are public records under Swedish law. Every date and title must match the employer's signed form. One inconsistency is enough to disqualify the application.
