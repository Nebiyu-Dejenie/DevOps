**Content (E2):** analytical and quantitative skills and programming logic; the fee is covered.

**Plan: 2 weeks, 1 hour a day.**

- **Days 1–4:** arrays, strings, loops, recursion and basic complexity, in Python.
- **Days 5–8:** logic puzzles, number sequences, percentages and rates.
- **Days 9–12:** timed mixed sets of 20 questions.
- **Days 13–14:** rest and review your mistakes.
