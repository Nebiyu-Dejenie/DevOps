> **Title:** Observability-driven reliability for digital-payment platforms in emerging markets
>
> **1. Background and motivation.** Ethiopia's payment system is moving quickly from cash to digital channels: card and QR payments at merchants, school-fee payments, and bank-integrated payment gateways. When a payment service degrades, merchants lose sales and customers lose trust in digital money. In my experience, most failures are noticed late, after users complain, even though warning signs were present in system metrics and logs. This gap between the data we collect and the decisions we take is the problem I want to study.

> **3. Research questions.**
> (1) Which combinations of metrics and log features best predict service degradation in transaction-processing systems before users are affected?
> (2) How can such detection run with modest computing resources, as is typical for fintech companies in developing countries?
> (3) How should alerts be designed so engineers act on them quickly without being overwhelmed by false alarms?
>
> **4. Method.** Year 1: core courses in [⏳ e.g. distributed systems, machine learning, data mining, network security, from the programme curriculum]. Evaluate them by detection lead time, false-alarm rate and resource cost.
>
> **5. Why China and why Dalian University of Technology.** China operates some of the world's largest digital-payment systems at extremely high transaction volumes, and Chinese universities research large-scale distributed systems and reliability engineering. After graduating I will return to Ethiopia's financial-technology sector to apply these methods, and to share them with engineers and students through workshops [⏳ only a real plan]. # 3. Academic recommendation briefing (not letters)

CSC requires **two recommendation letters from professors or associate professors**. Give each referee the facts below. **They write and sign the letter themselves**; do not write it for them.

| Item to give the referee | Content |
|---|---|
| Your CV and transcript | Latest versions |
| Programme and scholarship | University, programme, CSC Type A/B, deadline |
| What they can speak to | Final-project supervisor: your role and independence in the project (A−/A−). A− course lecturer: your performance in that course |
| Format | University letterhead, signature, date, contact details. Many universities ask for the letter in a sealed envelope or uploaded by the referee: check each university |
| Deadline | **15 Jan 2027** (before the earliest Type B deadline) |
