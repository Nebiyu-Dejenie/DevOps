**Rule:**

- Nothing fake: no bought or invented certificates, publications, projects, numbers or recommendation texts.
- If something is not finished, it does not go on an application as finished.

# Before the deadlines (now – January 2027)

| # | Improvement | Why it matters | Effort / cost | Strengthens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two public demo repositories** (CI/CD + Ansible; monitoring + alerts), built by you from scratch, no employer code | Reviewers can open them; they prove programming and engineering beyond the GPA | About 5 evenings / free | CMU (programming), AISS (portfolio), SMACCs (ICT), DUT |
| 2 | **1–2 real, manager-confirmed results** from SantimPay (e.g. incidents handled, automation time saved, services operated) | Turns duties into evidence; the strongest single improvement | One conversation with your manager / free | All applications, SI forms |
| 3 | **Final-year project one-pager** (title, problem, your part, screenshots) | Your best academic evidence (A−/A−) | 2 hours / free | AISS, EDISS, CSC, CMU |
| 4 | **One documented leadership example**, confirmed by the person who can sign the SI leadership form | SI and the Mastercard Foundation score leadership explicitly | 1 hour + a signature | SI, CMU |
| 5 | **Certificate IDs and verification links** for CKA (expired 2025), Azure Fundamentals, Fortinet NSE 1–3 | Unverifiable certificates weaken trust; verified ones help even when expired (list them as expired) | 1 hour / free | CVs |
| 6 | **One community session**: a free 2-hour "Linux and CI/CD basics" workshop for DTU or local students (real, with a date and attendee count) | Genuine giving back, with evidence (event post, photos, organiser contact) | 1 day / free | CMU and Mastercard Foundation, SMACCs, AISS |
| 7 | **One short technical write-up** of what you learned building demo 2 (blog or GitHub README) | Shows communication and reflection; supports interviews | 3 hours / free | AISS, SMACCs |
| 8 | **Optional: a discrete-mathematics or linear-algebra online course**, completed with a certificate | Addresses your weakest area honestly; mention it only once completed | 3–6 weeks / free to audit, fee for the certificate | EDISS, Swedish programme selection, interviews |
| 9 | **Optional: 1–2 small open-source contributions** (documentation or a bug fix) to a tool you use, such as an Ansible collection or a Prometheus exporter | Public, verifiable collaboration; list it only once merged | Variable / free | CMU, AISS |

**Do 1–4 first.** They need no money and change your applications the most. 6 and 7 are high value if you have the time. 8 and 9 are optional.

# Longer term (next cycle and career)

- A genuine research output (e.g. a workshop paper on anomaly detection in payment-platform monitoring) written with an academic supervisor.
- A current cloud or security certification (a hands-on exam) when your budget allows. Renewing the Kubernetes certification costs money; it is not needed for the 2027 deadlines.
- A formal team-lead or project-lead role at work, documented in writing.
- Calculus and statistics courses with grades: they open grade-screened programmes (e.g. Linköping, KTH, EDISS-type admissions).
- Sustained open-source contribution.

# What not to do

- Do not list "in progress" courses or exams as completed.
- Do not claim AI or research expertise; describe what you want to learn.
- Do not ask referees to sign letters you wrote. Give them the briefing pack and let them write.
