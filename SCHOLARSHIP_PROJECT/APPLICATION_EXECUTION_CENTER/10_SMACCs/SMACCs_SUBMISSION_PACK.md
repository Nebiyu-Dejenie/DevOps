# 3. Fit and path choice

**Path to choose:**

- **ICT specialisation:** Semester 2 at IHU, Thessaloniki, with the thesis at IHU.
- **Alternatives:** paths whose Semester 2 is at Mons, or a thesis at Vaasa (Smart Grid Solutions, if its monitoring and data side suits you).

| Smart-city theme | Genuine link to your work (only if confirmed) | What you must not claim |
|---|---|---|

| Smart urban services | Digital payments are city services: merchants, schools, transport fares, utility bills. School-fee payments are part of SantimPay's services | Running smart-city projects |
| Intelligent systems | Monitoring and alerting on live services; interest in anomaly detection | AI expertise (you have one introductory AI course, grade B) |

| Technology infrastructure | Reliability, automation and incident response | – |
| Sustainable digital transformation | Ethiopia's move from cash to digital payments; energy cost of running infrastructure | Energy-engineering knowledge |

**Weaknesses:**

- Grades (study success) and the lack of R&D experience or publications.

**Strengths:**

- Relevant degree content (CS → ICT track).
- Work experience on live public-facing digital services.
- An English-taught degree.
