## 3a. Study plan / research proposal

**Use this as a starting point.** Rewrite it in your own words and adjust the programme names to each university. Every `[⏳]` must be true before you submit.

> **Title:** Observability-driven reliability for digital-payment platforms in emerging markets
>
> **1. Background and motivation.** Ethiopia's payment system is moving quickly from cash to digital channels: card and QR payments at merchants, school-fee payments, and bank-integrated payment gateways. Since [⏳ year] I have worked at SantimPay Financial Solution S.C., a fintech that provides such services together with Ethiopian banks [⏳ verified role]. My work involves keeping these services available: operating Linux servers, deployment pipelines, configuration automation and monitoring [⏳ verified tools]. When a payment service degrades, merchants lose sales and customers lose trust in digital money. In my experience, most failures are noticed late, after users complain, even though warning signs were present in system metrics and logs. This gap between the data we collect and the decisions we take is the problem I want to study.
>
> **2. Academic preparation.** I hold a BSc in Computer Science from Debre Tabor University (2022). My strongest results came in my final year (GPA 3.20), including A− in Advanced Programming, Compiler Design, Complexity Theory and both parts of my final-year project ([⏳ title]). I also studied Introduction to Distributed Systems, Computer Security, Data Communication and Computer Networks, and Database Systems. I am aware that my mathematical foundation is weaker than my programming and systems background, and I will strengthen it during the first year through the programme's core courses [⏳ name them from the programme page].
>
> **3. Research questions.**
> (1) Which combinations of metrics and log features best predict service degradation in transaction-processing systems before users are affected?
> (2) How can such detection run with modest computing resources, as is typical for fintech companies in developing countries?
> (3) How should alerts be designed so engineers act on them quickly without being overwhelmed by false alarms?
>
> **4. Method.** Year 1: core courses in [⏳ e.g. distributed systems, machine learning, data mining, network security, from the programme curriculum]. Year 2, under my supervisor's guidance: build a test environment that replicates a small payment-processing service (open-source components only, no proprietary data), inject realistic faults (latency, resource exhaustion, dependency failures), collect metrics and logs, and compare detection approaches, from statistical thresholds to lightweight machine-learning models. Evaluate them by detection lead time, false-alarm rate and resource cost.
>
> **5. Why China and why Dalian University of Technology.** China operates some of the world's largest digital-payment systems at extremely high transaction volumes, and Chinese universities research large-scale distributed systems and reliability engineering. [⏳ One sentence naming a DUT School of Computer Science and Technology laboratory or professor whose work relates to your topic, taken from cs.dlut.edu.cn] Studying in an English-taught programme will let me learn from this experience directly.
>
> **6. Expected outcome and plan after graduation.** I expect to produce a master's thesis evaluating practical early-warning methods for payment-platform failures, and an open-source test environment others can reuse. After graduating I will return to Ethiopia's financial-technology sector to apply these methods, and to share them with engineers and students through workshops [⏳ only a real plan]. Reliable digital payments support financial inclusion, which is a shared development goal of Ethiopia and China.
>
> **7. Language and adaptation.** My bachelor's degree was taught in English [⏳ IELTS result if taken]. I will also study basic Chinese during my first year to take part fully in university life.
