# 3. Where not to spend money

- **NPU:** academic rule not met. Do not apply.
- **HUST:** do not pay the fee until it answers in writing.
- **KFUPM, Memorial, Regina, Ireland Fellows, Manaaki, MEXT university route:** published rules not met (see the Command Center "Eliminated" list).
- **IELTS:** only one test, and only because AISS requires it (an Ethiopian degree is not exempt, E1). If you drop AISS **and** EDISS, re-check whether any remaining programme needs it.

# 4. Other Erasmus Mundus and funded research options

- **Erasmus Mundus limit:** at most 3 applications per intake. Your three are AISS, SMACCs and EDISS (optional). No further Erasmus Mundus programme is recommended.
- **CDE** needs Earth-observation/GIS evidence you do not have.
- **CyberMACS:** its 2027 intake is unconfirmed.
- **Funded research master's:** only Manitoba and Calgary showed published funding rules that fit; both need a supervisor first. Drafts are in `Research_MSc_Canada/`.
