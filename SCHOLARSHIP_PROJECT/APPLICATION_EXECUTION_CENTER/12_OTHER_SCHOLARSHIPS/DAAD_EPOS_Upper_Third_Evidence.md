# 3. Where 2.62 stands (indicative only)

- **Modified Bavarian formula** (the method German universities commonly use):

  German grade = 1 + 3 × (best − yours) ÷ (best − minimum pass)

  = 1 + 3 × (4.00 − 2.62) ÷ (4.00 − 2.00) = **3.07**

- On the German scale, 1.0 is the best grade and 4.0 is a pass. **3.1 is "satisfactory"**, not "far above average".
- **Assumption:** the minimum passing CGPA is **2.00**, which is usual in Ethiopia but **not yet confirmed for DTU**. The registrar's grading-scale letter will confirm it.
- **The official conversion is made by the university or uni-assist, not by us.** This figure is only a warning sign.
- **What could still work:** class rank is about your position in **your** cohort. If the Extension-programme cohort's grades were low overall, 2.62 could still rank in the upper third. **Only DTU's records can show this.**

# 4. What DTU can issue (to confirm with the Registrar)

| Document | What it states | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Class rank letter** (best) | "ranked **X** out of **Y** graduates of BSc Computer Science (Extension programme), graduating July 2022" | Upper third if X ≤ Y ÷ 3 |
| Percentile statement | "in the top __ % of the graduating class" | Upper third if within the top 33 % |
| Cohort grade distribution | Number of graduates in each CGPA band | Lets the university judge your position |
| Grading scale and minimum pass | Scale and the minimum CGPA to graduate (already requested) | Needed for any German grade conversion |
