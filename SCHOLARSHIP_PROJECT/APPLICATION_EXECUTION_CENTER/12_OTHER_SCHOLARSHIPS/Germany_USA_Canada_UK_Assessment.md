**Bottom line:**

- For a **September 2027 start**, none of these four countries has an open, fully funded master's scholarship whose published rules you clearly meet.
- The realistic routes are all in the **next cycle (2028 start)**:
  - **Chevening (UK)**
  - **Mastercard Foundation Scholars at ASU (USA)**: its current phase focuses on innovation and technology
  - **Fulbright (USA)**
  - **DAAD EPOS (Germany)**: only if DTU's rank letter shows the upper third
- **Partial funding in Canada** remains possible if a professor agrees to supervise you.
- **Where you study is not where the funder is.** The Mastercard Foundation is based in Toronto (Canada) but funds study in Rwanda, the USA, Canada and elsewhere. The column "Funder" keeps the two apart.
- Nothing below is invented. Where a rule is not met, it says so.
