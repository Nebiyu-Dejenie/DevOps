**Rules:**

- **Nothing is sent without your approval.** You send every e-mail yourself, from your own address.
- **No funding is assumed** beyond what the university states in writing.
- Professors' research descriptions come from the universities' official pages (**E1**, read 7–8 Nov 2026).
- Before writing to anyone, open their profile, read **one recent paper or project**, and fill the `[⏳ paper]` line. A generic e-mail is worse than none.

**Tracker:** `Supervisor_Contact_Tracker.xlsx`. One row per professor. It calculates follow-up dates and shows which e-mails are waiting.

# 3. E-mail drafts (customise; send only after approval)

- Subject line names the topic.
- Body under 170 words.
- One professor per e-mail.
- Attach your CV (PDF) and transcript.
- Do not attach large files.
- Never write "Dear Sir/Madam" to a professor.

# 4. Follow-up rules

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| No reply after **7 days** (Manitoba) or **10 days** (Calgary) | One short follow-up in the same thread, then stop |
| Positive reply | Ask what the professor needs (CV, proposal, call); reply within 24 hours |
| "Not taking students" | Thank them; ask whether a colleague might be interested |
| Interview call | Prepare 2 minutes on your experience, 2 on your topic, 2 questions about their lab |
| Manitoba support letter | Enter the professor's details where the application requests the supervisor letter |

# 5. Order of work (if you decide "go" by 12 Nov)

1. **Today–10 Nov:** read the profiles and one paper each for M1, C1, C2, C3; fill the `[⏳]` lines.
2. **Send** M1 (Manitoba, urgent) and C1–C3. Log each in the tracker.
3. **17 Nov:** follow up on M1 if no reply.
4. **By 25 Nov:** Manitoba support secured, or drop Manitoba.
5. **Dec–Jan:** Calgary application listing the professors who replied positively (or the best-fitting ones).
