# Hungary: Stipendium Hungaricum, Application Package v1

**Status (E1, 7 Nov 2026):** "Application is closed for the 2026/27 academic year". The **2027/28 call is not yet published**. Last cycle ran 14 Nov 2025 – **15 Jan 2026, 14:00 CET**. Expect a similar window (an assumption, not confirmed).

## 1. Verified facts

| Item | Verified fact | Ev. |
|---|---|---|
| Ethiopia | Federal Democratic Republic of Ethiopia is a Sending Partner for full-time programmes. **You must be nominated** by the Ethiopian authority (Ministry responsible for higher education). Sending partners may set **earlier** deadlines | E1 call 2026/27 |
| Funding (2026/27) | Tuition-free; **HUF 43,700/month** (contribution); free dorm **or** HUF 40,000/month; medical insurance. A Hungarian language course is compulsory in year 1 (the stipend is affected if you skip it) | E1 |
| Process | DreamApply application → technical check and nomination → institutional admission (exam or interview Mar–May) → results June/July → visa → arrival by 30 Sept | E1 |

## 3. Documents checklist (based on programme pages; confirm in the 2027/28 call)

- [ ] Degree certificate and full transcript. Szeged asks for notarized English translations; your documents are already in English, so ask whether certified copies suffice.
- [ ] Proof of English (B2): IELTS, or for ELTE "any certificate". Ask whether a medium-of-instruction letter from DTU is accepted.
- [ ] Passport copy (the page with name, photo, number and expiry, as required by the university).
- [ ] **Medical certificate**, not older than 1 month (Szeged).
- [ ] CV (≤ 2 pages).
- [ ] Motivation letter (below).
- [ ] Recommendation letters: optional at Szeged (2 from academics).
- [ ] Course syllabuses: Szeged "might" request them. Included in the Department request letter.
