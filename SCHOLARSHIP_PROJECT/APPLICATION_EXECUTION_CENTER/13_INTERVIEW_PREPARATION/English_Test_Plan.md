# Decision

**Take one IELTS Academic, computer-delivered, at a test centre:**

- **Where:** British Council, Addis Ababa University (6 Kilo)
- **Fee:** ETB 32,800 (E1, ielts.org)
- **Target:** **7.0 overall, no band below 6.5**
- **Do not take:**
  - **IELTS Online**: EDISS rejects it
  - **TOEFL**: more expensive and adds no programme

**Total planned cost: ETB 32,800.**

# 3. Timing (earliest deadline first)

**Results:**

- Most computer-delivered IELTS results are issued **within 2 days**; about half within 1 day (E1, ielts.org global data).

**Deadlines that drive the plan:**

- EDISS needs the **Test Report Form number in the form by 12 Jan 2027**.
- AISS needs the result by **2 Feb 2027**.

| If… | Book by | Test date | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manitoba = **go** | **13 Nov** | **by 24 Nov** | Results in about 2 days, before the 1 Dec deadline |
| Manitoba = **no** (recommended unless a supervisor agrees this week) | **20 Nov** | **1–12 Dec** | Leaves time for one retake before EDISS's 12 Jan deadline |

**Retake buffer:**

- If one band is below 6.5, ask the centre whether **IELTS One Skill Retake** is available for computer-delivered tests in Addis (it exists on ielts.org; local availability not confirmed).
- Otherwise retake the full test by **5 Jan**.

# 4. Four-week study plan (9 Nov – test week)

Daily: about 90 minutes on weekdays, 3 hours on Saturday and Sunday. The question bank is in `English_Test_and_Interview_Plan_v1`.

| Week | Focus | Concrete tasks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (9–15 Nov) | Diagnostic + test format | One full official practice test **on a computer**. Record band per skill. Learn the computer interface (highlighting, word count). Book the test |
| 2 (16–22 Nov) | Writing + Reading | 3 Task 1 reports (charts, processes) and 3 Task 2 essays, timed (20 + 40 min). Rewrite each after feedback. Reading: 2 passages a day, 20 min each; practise True/False/Not Given and headings |
| 3 (23–29 Nov) | Listening + Speaking | 2 listening sections a day. Speaking: record Parts 1–3 daily on work, education and technology. Remove fillers; extend answers with reasons and examples |
| 4 (30 Nov – test) | Full mocks | 2 full timed mocks on a computer; review only your weakest skill; sleep and logistics (ID, arrival time) |

**Keep your passport valid and the name identical** on the booking, test and every application.
