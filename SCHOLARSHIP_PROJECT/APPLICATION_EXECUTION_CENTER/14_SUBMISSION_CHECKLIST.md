# Before ANY submission

- ☐ Name identical to the passport on every page and file
- ☐ GPA entered as **2.62 / 4.00**, exactly as printed
- ☐ Every date and title identical to the HR letters
- ☐ Every essay in your own words and within the word limit
- ☐ No `[⏳ …]` placeholder and no "Generator check" footer left anywhere
- ☐ Referees know the deadline and will answer e-mails
- ☐ PDFs readable at 100%, upright, under the size limit
- ☐ A screenshot or PDF of the confirmation saved

# 1. CMU-Africa MSIT + Mastercard Foundation: **submit by 12 Dec** (deadline 15 Dec 2026)

- ☐ Portal account created in Chrome or Firefox
- ☐ Essay questions pasted into `07_CMU_AFRICA/CMU-Africa_SUBMISSION_PACK` and answered
- ☐ CV ≤ **2 pages** as a PDF (`06_FINAL_CV/CV_CMU-Africa_2pages`)
- ☐ Unofficial transcript + grading key, all years in **one** PDF
- ☐ 3 recommenders entered (supervisor, A− lecturer, SantimPay manager)
- ☐ Video essay practised (it **cannot be re-recorded**)
- ☐ After submission: financial aid form; technical test when invited

# 2. Sweden (universityadmissions.se + SI)

- ☐ 4 programmes chosen from `08_SWEDEN_SI/SI_Programme_Shortlist.xlsx`, all on SI's 2027/28 list
- ☐ Programmes ranked by real preference; entry requirements confirmed
- ☐ **SEK 900 paid** after the programme check (fee deadline 1 Feb)
- ☐ DTU has e-mailed the transcript to UHR; diploma + passport uploaded **by 1 Feb**
- ☐ SI portal (8–24 Feb, **14:59 CET**):
    - SI-template CV (≤ 3 pages)
    - work + leadership forms (signed and stamped; one PDF; ≤ 5 pages)
    - 2 references (SI template)
    - motivation
    - "SI hours - no double count" sheet shows **MEETS**

# 3. SMACCs: **submit by 25 Jan** (deadline 1 Feb 2027)

- ☐ Facts confirmed on smaccs.eu (application, instructions, deadlines pages)
- ☐ Path chosen: ICT specialisation (IHU) + 2 alternative paths
- ☐ Motivation letter (`10_SMACCs`), CV (`06_FINAL_CV/CV_Europe_Academic`)
- ☐ 2 reference letters, **at least 1 academic**
- ☐ English proof (IELTS, or English-medium letter if SMACCs confirms it)

# 4. AISS: **submit by 30 Jan** (deadline 2 Feb 2027)

- ☐ Video ≤ **1:30**, as a link; you on screen at least half the time; own voice
- ☐ Portfolio: one PDF of up to 5 summaries (`09_AISS/AISS_Portfolio_Summaries`)
- ☐ CV; education documents; passport page
- ☐ IELTS (6.5, each part ≥ 5.5)
- ☐ **Self-funding plan** (mandatory)
- ☐ "Erasmus Mundus scholarship" box ticked

# 5. China CSC: DUT Type B (deadline 15 Feb 2027)

- ☐ DUT confirmed an English-taught CS master's and its agency number (e-mail 2)
- ☐ iso.dlut.edu.cn application done; DUT application number noted
- ☐ CSC online application: **Type B**, DUT agency number
- ☐ Study plan, 2 professor letters, notarised degree and transcript, medical form, police clearance, English proof
- ☐ **CNY 400** paid only after the above
