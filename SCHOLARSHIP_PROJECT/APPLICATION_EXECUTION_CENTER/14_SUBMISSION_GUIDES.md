**Evidence codes:**

- **E1:** read on the official site
- **E2:** found by search; check in your browser

# 0. Copy-paste blocks used in every portal (verified facts only)

**Name:** exactly as in your passport (you hold this privately; it is not stored in these files).

**Education entry:**

> Institution: Debre Tabor University, Faculty of Technology (Ethiopia)
> Degree: Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (Extension programme)
> Period: academic years 2017/18 – 2021/22 [⏳ start and end months exactly as on the transcript]
> Date awarded: 14 July 2022
> Cumulative GPA: 2.62 / 4.00
> Language of instruction: English

**Grading scale (when a field asks for it):**

> Debre Tabor University uses a 4.00 scale: A+ = 4.00, A = 4.00, A− = 3.75 (Excellent); B+ = 3.50, B = 3.00 (Very Good); B− = 2.75, C+ = 2.50 (Good); C = 2.00 (Satisfactory); C− = 1.75 (Unsatisfactory); D = 1.00 (Very Poor); Fx/F = 0 (Fail). Highest grade A+/A (4.00); lowest grade F (0). D and above are passing grades. Cumulative GPA 2.62 out of 4.00.

**GPA fields:**

- Always enter **2.62 / 4.00 as printed**.
- **Never** enter the final-year 3.20 in a GPA field. It is your own calculation and belongs only in essays, labelled as such.

**Employment entries:** only after the HR letters, with titles and dates **copied exactly** from them.

**File names:**

- Use `Surname_Givenname_Document.pdf` (e.g. `Surname_Givenname_Transcript.pdf`) unless a portal sets its own rule.
- EDISS sets its own rule: `lastname-firstname-document.pdf`.

**Before any upload:**

- PDF, not a photo
- all pages upright
- readable at 100%
- under the portal's size limit
- stamps and signatures visible

# 9. Final check before pressing Submit (any portal)

1. Name identical to the passport in every field and file.
2. Dates identical to the HR letters and the transcript.
3. GPA 2.62/4.00 exactly as printed.
4. Every essay within its word limit, in your own words.
5. Referees warned, with the deadline in their calendar.
6. A screenshot or PDF of the submission confirmation saved in `13_FINAL_SUBMISSIONS/<programme>/`.
7. **Your own explicit decision to submit.**
