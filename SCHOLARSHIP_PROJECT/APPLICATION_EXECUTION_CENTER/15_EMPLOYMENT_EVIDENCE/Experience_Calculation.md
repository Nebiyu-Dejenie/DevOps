# 4. How the calculation works

- **One row per job title and period**, copied from the HR letter. A row counts only if:
  - the evidence is **E0 or E1**, and
  - start, end, hours/week and weeks/year are all filled in.

  Your confirmation (E4) and CV text (E5) never count.
- **SI's formula** is hours/week × weeks/year × years (E1). The workbook applies the same weekly rate to the days in each calendar month: hours/week × (weeks worked ÷ 52) × days ÷ 7.
- **Each month counted once:** in a month with two jobs, only the job with more hours counts. SI does not say whether jobs held at the same time may be added. Chevening's portal adds entries. The "added" figure is shown beside the safe figure, and decisions use the safe one.
- **Windows:**
  - SI: everything before 8 Feb 2027.
  - Chevening: after 14 Jul 2022, up to the 2028/29 deadline (assumed 6 Oct 2027; replace it when announced).
  - EPOS: after 14 Jul 2022, up to your course's deadline (assumed 31 Aug 2027).
- **Types:**
  - Internships and voluntary work are excluded for SI (E1).
  - Chevening counts every type (E1).
  - EPOS excludes internships and voluntary work, and needs "related = Y". This is an assumption, because EPOS asks for professional experience.
- **Current job:** enter the HR letter's date as the end date. When SI's own form is signed in February 2027, update the end date to the form's date.
