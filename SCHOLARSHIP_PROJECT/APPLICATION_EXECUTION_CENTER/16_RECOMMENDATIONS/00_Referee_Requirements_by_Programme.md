**Integrity rule:**

- The drafts in this folder are **starting points for your referees, not finished letters**.
- Each referee must change them to reflect their own knowledge and opinion, replace every `[Referee: …]` field, and sign only what they genuinely believe.
- Some programmes check letters for applicant authorship. A letter written by the applicant and signed unchanged can lead to rejection.
